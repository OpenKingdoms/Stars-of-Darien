# Asset credits

Models in Stars of Darien are made for the project, or made from assets that anyone may use. This page lists the assets from elsewhere that went into them.

| Asset | Used in | By | Licence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human Base Meshes 1.4.1: the realistic head (`GEO-head_animation_realistic`) and the place and size of its eyes | Elsin's face in `unity/Assets/Overrides/Units/ARAKING.glb`, made by `tools/units/elsin_head.py` | Blender Studio and community contributors | CC0 1.0 | [blender.org demo files](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) |
| Human Base Meshes 1.4.1: the realistic head (`GEO-head_animation_realistic`) with its sculpted detail and the place and size of its eyes, and the realistic skull (`Skull - Realistic`) | Lokken's face, drawn tight over the skull, and the skull on his staff, in `unity/Assets/Overrides/Units/TARNECRO.glb`, made by `tools/units/lokken_head.py` and `tools/units/lokken_robes.py` | Blender Studio and community contributors | CC0 1.0 | [blender.org demo files](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) |
| Human Base Meshes 1.4.1: the head of the realistic female body (`GEO-body_female_realistic`) with its sculpted detail, and the place and size of its eyes | Kirenna's face in `unity/Assets/Overrides/Units/VERMAGE.glb`, made by `tools/units/kirenna_head.py` | Blender Studio and community contributors | CC0 1.0 | [blender.org demo files](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) |

The kits in `tools/` download these assets when they run and keep them outside the repository. The rest of every model, its shapes and its pictures, is made by the kits from numbers and the model's own shape.
