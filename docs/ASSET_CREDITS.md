# Asset credits

Models in Stars of Darien are made for the project, or made from assets that anyone may use. This page lists the assets from elsewhere that went into them.

| Asset | Used in | By | Licence | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human Base Meshes 1.4.1: the realistic head (`GEO-head_animation_realistic`) and the place and size of its eyes | Elsin's face in `unity/Assets/Overrides/Units/ARAKING.glb`, made by `tools/units/elsin_head.py` | Blender Studio and community contributors | CC0 1.0 | [blender.org demo files](https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/) |

The kits in `tools/` download these assets when they run and keep them outside the repository. The rest of every model, its shapes and its pictures, is made by the kits from numbers and the model's own shape.
