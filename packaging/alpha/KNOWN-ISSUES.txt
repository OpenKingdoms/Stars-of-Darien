Known issues in Stars of Darien, @VERSION@

This list is for playtesters. It says what is not in this alpha yet and the
rough edges we already know about, so you can spend your time on the rest.
If you hit something on this list and it hurts more than it says here, tell
us anyway.


NOT IN THIS ALPHA YET

- Multiplayer. The Play an Opponent door is shut. Skirmish against the
  computer is the whole of this alpha.
- The campaign. The Play the Adventure door only says it comes later.
- The map editor and Load game on the main menu. To load a saved game, open
  the skirmish screen and press Load there.
- A score screen after the battle. Victory or defeat shows the kingdoms and
  their state, without units built, kills or a score.
- Veteran ranks and kill counts on units.
- Game speeds other than Normal and Fast, and a pause key in battle. F1
  opens the game menu, which stops the battle.
- Separate volume for music, sound and unit voices, and a resolution
  setting. The game runs at your desktop's resolution.
- Key rebinding, chat and the typed + commands of the original.
- Replays.


ROUGH EDGES WE KNOW ABOUT

- The battle stops while the game's window is in the background, and so
  does the sound.
- In a big battle with many computers, the game can still pause for a
  moment once in a while.
- Loading a large map takes up to about 10 seconds on a fast PC, and
  longer on a slow one or the first time after Windows starts.


WHEN YOU REPORT A BUG

Say what you did and what happened, and attach Player.log from

    %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\OpenKingdoms\Stars of Darien

with a screenshot (F9) if you have one. README.txt has more on where things
are.
